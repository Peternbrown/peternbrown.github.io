What I Own

Internal Project Management Platform Rebuild

When our team's project management software license wasn't renewed, we were left without a system overnight. Rather than wait on a replacement vendor, I took ownership of the problem and built a custom application from scratch using Microsoft Power Apps.

I led requirements gathering through focus groups and in-depth meetings with all three sub-teams that would use the tool, translated those findings into a product plan, then designed and built the application end to end — from first requirement to launch.

What I Owned

  • Led discovery across 3 sub-teams through focus groups and 1:1 stakeholder meetings
  • Synthesized findings into requirements and a build plan
  • Designed and built the full application in Power Apps, solo, from zero to launch
  • Built and continue to maintain the product backlog, shipping fixes and improvements post-launch

Stack

Microsoft Power Apps Focus Groups Requirements Gathering Backlog Management
Impact: Delivered a production-ready, purpose-built replacement in weeks — live across all 3 sub-teams, at zero ongoing licensing cost, and better fit to the team's actual workflow than the tool it replaced.
